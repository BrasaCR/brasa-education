How the education catalog is counted.

Publication rules

  • Every number needs a definition, source URL, review date and verification label.
  • Provider-reported numbers are attributed to the provider.
  • Derived totals disclose possible overlap.
  • Language counts distinguish documentation, interface translation and course-language availability.
  • Expired or unsupported claims are removed rather than silently carried forward.

Resource transparency

Every governed resource record must show source, provider, review date, language, cost, credential status, curriculum relevance, accessibility information and offline suitability. Missing information remains visibly marked instead of being inferred.
