Operating principle

AI recommends; educators decide.

BRASA can surface patterns and next-step suggestions. It does not diagnose children, assign irreversible labels, or replace teachers. Every intervention remains reviewable by authorized school staff.

Child safety

Controls before collection

  • Role-based access for administrators, teachers, guardians and learners
  • Verified school authority before institutional access
  • Purpose-specific consent with withdrawal and expiration
  • No Khan Academy passwords collected by BRASA
  • No public display of phone numbers, birth dates or learning histories
  • Human review before any AI-assisted intervention

Portable education history

The learner remains central

  • Exportable, readable education record
  • Evidence provenance and teacher verification
  • School-transfer continuity without surrendering ownership
  • Correction and removal requests
  • Revocable school and guardian relationships
  • Minimum-data design with audit history
Meeting decision

Policies are agreed before real students enter.

The school and BRASA should jointly approve consent language, retention periods, safeguarding escalation, authorized roles, record transfer and acceptable AI use before the pilot begins.

Controlled pilot

Small enough to learn safely

Agree success measures

Mastery growth, participation, teacher time, intervention effectiveness and family understanding.

Use demonstration records first

Train staff and test workflows without real student information.

Begin with one consenting class

A limited term, a limited subject set and named authorized staff.

Review every week

Teachers validate recommendations, document problems and can stop the pilot.

Decide with evidence

Expand, revise or end the pilot based on agreed outcomes—not enrollment pressure.

Pilot safeguards

Launch gates

  • School authority verified
  • Named safeguarding lead
  • Guardian and learner consent approved
  • Access and incident procedures tested
  • Export and deletion tested
  • Teacher training completed
  • Baseline and review date recorded

No launch gate is currently marked complete. These are meeting decisions, not assumptions.
