# BRASA Education

**Sekolo se seng le se seng, boemo bo bong le bo bong, moithuti e mong le e mong—e hokahane le naha ka naha.**

[Bula BRASA Education](https://brasa.education/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Mmuso](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Khoebo](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA Education e thusa baahi ho fumana likolo, liunivesithi, lithuto, litifikeiti, le lisebelisoa tsa ho ithuta mahala. Naha ka 'ngoe e fumana leqephe la eona la thuto le bukana ea sekolo, e hokahaneng le leqephe le ka sehloohong la naha eo la BRASA.

## Seo baahi ba ka se fumanang

- Likolo tsa sechaba le tsa poraefete moo ho nang le lintlha tse netefalitsoeng
- Liunivesithi le thuto ea theknoloji
- Lithuto le litifikeiti tsa mahala
- Mekhoa ea ho ithuta bakeng sa bana le batho ba baholo
- Litsi tsa thuto tse ikhethileng naheng le lisebelisoa tsa semmuso

## Libukana tsa likolo tsa naha

Litlaleho tsa sekolo li tsoa mehloling e ngotsoeng ea sechaba kapa ea setsi. BRASA e tlaleha matsatsi a ho qala le ho hlahloba, e qoba ho qapa likolo tse sieo, 'me e ntlafatsa li-database tsa naha ka boikemelo.

## Ho fihlella puo

Senyesemane e ntse e le mohloli o tsitsitseng. Maqephe a sebakeng sa heno a sebelisa li-URL tse arohaneng 'me a hlahlojoa bakeng sa mantsoe a thuto, mabitso a sekolo, mangolo, tataiso le mokhoa oa ho fumaneha.

## Qala ho ithuta

Etela [brasa.education](https://brasa.education/) kapa qala leqepheng la naha ea heno ho [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/).

## Kenya letsoho

Thusa ho tseba lethathamo la boitsebiso la sekolo, ho netefatsa bukana ea sekolo, ho hlahloba phetolelo, kapa ho tlaleha sehokelo sa thuto se siiloeng ke nako.

## BRASA kajeno

BRASA jwale e hokahanya dibaka tsa dinaha tse 198 le Thuto, Mmuso le Kgwebo. BRASA Business e na le dikarolo tse 12 tse hokahaneng: thekiso le dirasiti, CRM, setoko, batho le thupelo, meputso le dibanka, nako, tshehetso ya lelapa, molao, diforomo tsa mmuso, ditlaleho le sebaka se kopanetsweng sa mosebetsi. E boetse e kenyelletsa lejara e bulehileng, ditsamaiso tse tataiswang le tshebetso ntle le inthanete.

BRASA Financials e thathamisa ditsamaiso tsa tefo tse 385 ho tswa direkotong tse 395, ka ditlhaloso tsa mebaraka tse 161 le mefuta ya tefo e 216: tjhelete ya mohala, phetisetso ya hanghang, QR, dipotlana tsa dijithale, marangrang a tjhelete, remitense, open banking, dikarete tsa lehae le USSD.

Ho ba lethathamong ha ho bolele hore ho se ho butswe. Bafani ba nang le laesense ke bona ba tsamaisang tjhelete; mokgwa o mong le o mong o hloka tlhahlobo ya naha le mohlodi, laesense, kopanyo, teko ya tefo le tumello ya tlhahiso.
