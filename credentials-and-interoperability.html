Portable evidence

Credentials must say who decided what.

No certification claim. BRASA’s internal profile is designed toward Open Badges compatibility. BRASA does not claim 1EdTech certification, conformance, endorsement, or a live OneRoster or LTI integration.

Credential layers

  1. Participation record: evidence that an activity occurred.
  2. Teacher-verified evidence: a verified educator reviewed defined work against stated criteria.
  3. Institution-authorized credential: a verified institution issued within its documented authority.
  4. Externally recognized credential: recognition comes from the named external authority, never from BRASA’s description.

Open Badges direction

The internal portable achievement schema requires recipient, achievement criteria, evidence, issuer scope, issue date, verification method and revocation state. Formal conformance language remains prohibited until the applicable 1EdTech certification process is completed.

Interoperability boundaries

OneRoster
Future roster, class and enrollment exchange. BRASA’s own school records remain authoritative until a certified adapter is tested.
LTI
Future secure launch and learning-tool context exchange. An ordinary hyperlink is not described as an LTI integration.
Open Badges
Future portable achievement exchange. An exported BRASA evidence record is not automatically a recognized badge.

Reference: 1EdTech interoperability specifications.
