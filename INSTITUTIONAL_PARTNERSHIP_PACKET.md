# BRASA Education institutional partnership packet

This packet prevents a conversation, link or content listing from being misrepresented as a partnership.

## Due diligence

- Verify legal identity and signing authority.
- Record jurisdiction, regulatory status and official source.
- Identify the institution's authority over content, instruction, assessment, credentials and recognition.
- Review safeguarding, privacy, accessibility, security and incident obligations.
- Record effective date, expiration, territory, languages and termination rights.

## Claim controls

- “Listed” does not mean “partner.”
- “Partner” does not mean “accredited,” “authorized” or “recognized.”
- BRASA may describe only the signed scope while it is effective.
- Provider, institution, accreditor and government authority remain separately identified.
- Technical conformance claims require the applicable certification evidence.

## Required records

- Executed agreement and amendments
- Authority and regulatory evidence
- Data-processing and safeguarding terms
- Approved public wording
- Integration acceptance tests, where applicable
- Credential specimen and revocation process, where applicable
- Renewal and termination history

Gate 9 remains externally blocked until at least one qualified institution executes this packet and authorizes a defined production scope.
