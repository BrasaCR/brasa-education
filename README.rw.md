# BRASA Education

** Buri shuri, urwego rwose, buriwiga-uhuza igihugu nigihugu. **

[Fungura BRASA Education](https://brasa.education/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Guverinoma](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Ubucuruzi](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA Education bufasha abaturage kubona amashuri, kaminuza, amasomo, impamyabumenyi, hamwe nubushobozi bwo kwiga kubuntu. Buri gihugu cyakira urupapuro rwuburezi nubuyobozi bwishuri, bihujwe nurupapuro nyamukuru rwa BRASA.

## Icyo abenegihugu bashobora kubona

- Amashuri ya leta nayigenga ahari amakuru yagenzuwe arahari
- Kaminuza n'amashuri makuru
- Amasomo n'impamyabumenyi
- Inzira yo kwiga kubana nabakuze
- Ibigo byuburezi byihariye byigihugu hamwe nubutunzi

## Ubuyobozi bwishuri ryigihugu

Inyandiko zishuri ziva mubitabo rusange cyangwa ibigo. BRASA yandika amatariki yo gusuzuma no gusuzuma amatariki, irinda guhimba amashuri yabuze, no kuvugurura imibare yigihugu yigenga.

## Kubona ururimi

Icyongereza gikomeza kuba isoko. Impapuro zaho zikoresha URL zitandukanye kandi zirasubirwamo kubijyanye nuburezi, amazina yishuri, inyandiko, icyerekezo, hamwe nibishoboka.

## Tangira kwiga

Sura [brasa.education](https://brasa.education/) cyangwa utangire ku rupapuro rw’igihugu cyawe kuri [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/).

## Tanga umusanzu

Fasha kumenya dataset yishuri yemewe, kugenzura ububiko bwishuri, gusuzuma ibisobanuro, cyangwa gutanga raporo yuburezi bwashaje.

## BRASA uyu munsi

BRASA ubu ihuza aho ibihugu 198 bigana n'Uburezi, Guverinoma n'Ubucuruzi. BRASA Business ifite ibice 12 bihujwe: kugurisha n'inyemezabwishyu, CRM, ibarura, abakozi n'amahugurwa, imishahara na banki, igihe, ubufasha bw'umuryango, amategeko, impapuro za leta, raporo n'ahantu ho gukorera hamwe. Harimo kandi igitabo cy'ibaruramari gifunguye, inzira z'akazi ziyobowe no gukomeza gukora nta murandasi.

BRASA Financials ikataloge sisitemu 385 zo kwishyura zivuye mu nyandiko 395, zirimo ibisobanuro by'amasoko 161 n'ubwoko 216 bwo kwishyura: amafaranga ya telefoni, kohereza ako kanya, QR, amakofi y'ikoranabuhanga, imiyoboro y'amafaranga, kohereza amafaranga, open banking, amakarita yo mu gihugu na USSD.

Kuba muri kataloge ntibisobanura ko byatangiye gukora. Abatanga serivisi bafite uruhushya ni bo bimura amafaranga; buri buryo bugomba kugenzurwa ku gihugu n'inkomoko, uruhushya, ihuzwa, igerageza ry'ubwishyu n'icyemezo cyo gukoresha.
