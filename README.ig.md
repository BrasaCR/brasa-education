# BRASA Education

** Ụlọ akwụkwọ ọ bụla, ọkwa ọ bụla, onye mmụta ọ bụla - obodo jikọtara ya na obodo.**

[Mepee BRASA Education](https://brasa.education/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Ọchịchị](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Azụmahịa](https://brasa.business/)

BRASA Education na-enyere ụmụ amaala aka ịchọta ụlọ akwụkwọ, mahadum, nkuzi, asambodo na akụrụngwa mmụta n'efu. Obodo ọ bụla na-enweta ibe agụmakwụkwọ nke ya na akwụkwọ ndekọ aha ụlọ akwụkwọ, jikọtara na ibe BRASA isi obodo ahụ.

## Ihe ụmụ amaala nwere ike ịhụ

- Ụlọ akwụkwọ ọha na nkeonwe ebe data akwadoro dị
- Mahadum na nkuzi nka
- Nkuzi na asambodo efu
- Ụzọ mmụta maka ụmụaka na ndị okenye
- Ụlọ akwụkwọ agụmakwụkwọ nke obodo na akụrụngwa gọọmentị

## Akwụkwọ ndekọ aha ụlọ akwụkwọ obodo

Ihe ndekọ ụlọ akwụkwọ na-abịa site na isi mmalite ọha ma ọ bụ ụlọ ọrụ edekọtara. BRASA na-edekọ akara ngosi na ụbọchị nyocha, na-ezere imepụta ụlọ akwụkwọ na-efu efu, ma na-emelite ntọala data obodo n'adabereghị.

## Nweta asụsụ

Bekee ka bụ isi mmalite echekwara. Ibe ndị mpaghara na-eji URL dị iche iche ma na-enyocha ya maka nkọwa nkuzi, aha ụlọ akwụkwọ, scripts, ntụzịaka na nnweta.

## Malite mmụta

Gaa na [brasa.education](https://brasa.education/) ma ọ bụ malite na ibe obodo gị na [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/).

## Nye aka

Nyere aka ịchọpụta ihe ndekọ data ụlọ akwụkwọ gọọmentị, nyochaa akwụkwọ ndekọ aha ụlọ akwụkwọ, nyochaa ntụgharị asụsụ, ma ọ bụ kọọ njikọ agụmakwụkwọ emechiela.

* BRASA Education · Ọzụzụ efu maka ụmụ amaala nke ụwa.

## BRASA taa

BRASA na-ejikọta ugbu a ebe mba 198 na Mmụta, Gọọmenti na Azụmahịa. BRASA Business nwere ngalaba 12 jikọtara ọnụ: ire ahịa na akwụkwọ nnata, CRM, ngwa ahịa, ndị ọrụ na ọzụzụ, ụgwọ ọrụ na ụlọ akụ, oge, nkwado ezinụlọ, ọrụ iwu, fọm gọọmenti, akụkọ na ebe ọrụ ọnụ. Ọ gụnyekwara akwụkwọ ndekọ ego mepere emepe, usoro ọrụ a na-eduzi na ọrụ na-aga n'ihu n'enweghị ịntanetị.

BRASA Financials depụtara usoro ịkwụ ụgwọ 385 sitere na ndekọ 395, na-anọchi anya nkọwa ahịa 161 na ụdị ịkwụ ụgwọ 216: ego ekwentị, nnyefe ozugbo, QR, obere akpa dijitalụ, netwọkụ ego, nnyefe ego, open banking, kaadị obodo na USSD.

Ịdị na katalọgụ apụtaghị na arụnyere ya. Ndị na-enye ọrụ nwere ikike na-ebuga ego; usoro ọ bụla chọrọ nyocha mba na isi mmalite, ikike, njikọta, ule mmezi na nkwenye mmepụta. BRASA na-ejikọta nhọrọ ndị a kwadoro na akwụkwọ nnata, mmezi na akwụkwọ ndekọ ego mepere emepe.
