# BRASA Education

**Zonke izikole, wonke amazinga, wonke umfundi—kuxhunywe izwe ngezwe.**

[Open BRASA Education](https://brasa.education/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Uhulumeni](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Business](https://brasa.business/)

Imfundo ye-BRASA isiza izakhamizi ukuthi zithole izikole, amanyuvesi, izifundo, izitifiketi, kanye nezinsiza zokufunda zamahhala. Izwe ngalinye lithola ikhasi lalo lemfundo kanye nemibhalo yesikole, exhunywe ekhasini eliyinhloko le-BRASA lalelo zwe.

## Lokho izakhamizi ezingakuthola

- Izikole zikahulumeni nezizimele lapho idatha eqinisekisiwe itholakala khona
- Amanyuvesi kanye nemfundo yezobuchwepheshe
- Izifundo zamahhala nezitifiketi
- Izindlela zokufunda zezingane kanye nabantu abadala
- Izikhungo zemfundo eziqondene nezwe kanye nezinsiza ezisemthethweni

## Imibhalo yezikole zezwe

Amarekhodi esikole avela emithonjeni yomphakathi ebhaliwe noma yesikhungo. I-BRASA irekhoda izinsuku zokuqala nezinsuku zokubuyekeza, igwema ukusungula izikole ezingekho, futhi ibuyekeza amasethi edatha wezwe ngokuzimela.

## Ukufinyelela ulimi

IsiNgisi sihlala siwumthombo ogciniwe. Amakhasi enziwe endawo asebenzisa ama-URL ahlukene futhi abuyekezwa kumatemu ezemfundo, amagama ezikole, imibhalo, isiqondiso, nokufinyeleleka.

## Qala ukufunda

Vakashela [brasa.education](https://brasa.education/) noma qala ekhasini lakho lezwe ku-[BRASA World](https://brasa.world/).

## Faka isandla

Siza ukukhomba isethi yedatha yesikole esemthethweni, uqinisekise uhla lwemibhalo lwesikole, ubuyekeze ukuhumusha, noma ubike isixhumanisi semfundo esiphelelwe yisikhathi.

## BRASA namuhla

BRASA manje ixhumanisa izindawo zamazwe angu-198 neMfundo, uHulumeni kanye neBhizinisi. BRASA Business inezindawo eziyi-12 ezixhunyiwe: ukuthengisa namarisidi, CRM, impahla, abantu nokuqeqeshwa, amaholo namabhange, isikhathi, ukwesekwa komndeni, umsebenzi wezomthetho, amafomu kahulumeni, imibiko nendawo yokusebenza ehlanganyelwe. Ihlanganisa nebhuku lezimali elivulekile, izindlela zokusebenza eziqondisiwe nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

BRASA Financials ibala izinhlelo zokukhokha ezingama-385 ezivela kumarekhodi angama-395, ngezincazelo zemakethe eziyi-161 nezinhlobo zokukhokha ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudlulisa ngokushesha, QR, izikhwama zedijithali, amanethiwekhi emali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kanye ne-USSD.

Ukufakwa ohlwini akusho ukuthi sekuvuliwe. Abahlinzeki abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela ngayinye idinga ukubuyekezwa kwezwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukuhlanganiswa, ukuhlolwa kokukhokhelana kanye nemvume yokusebenza.
