# BRASA Education

**Isikolo ngasinye, inqanaba ngalinye, wonke umfundi—uqhagamshelwe kwilizwe ngalinye.**

[Vula i-BRASA Education](https://brasa.education/) · [BRASA World](https://brasa.world/) · [Urhulumente](https://brasagovernment.net/) · [Ishishini](https://brasa.business/)

Imfundo ye-BRASA inceda abemi bafumane izikolo, iiyunivesithi, iikhosi, izatifikethi, kunye nezixhobo zokufunda simahla. Ilizwe ngalinye lifumana iphepha lalo lemfundo kunye nencwadi yesikolo, eqhagamshelwe kwiphepha eliphambili le-BRASA yelo lizwe.

## Yintoni abemi banokuyifumana

- Izikolo zikarhulumente nezikolo zabucala apho idatha eqinisekisiweyo ifumaneka khona
- Iiyunivesithi kunye nemfundo yobugcisa
- Izifundo zasimahla kunye nezatifikethi
- Iindlela zokufunda zabantwana kunye nabantu abadala
- Amaziko emfundo ajongene nelizwe ngalinye kunye nezibonelelo ezisemthethweni

## Izalathisi zezikolo zelizwe

Iirekhodi zesikolo zivela kwimithombo ebhaliweyo kawonke-wonke okanye yeziko. I-BRASA irekhoda imihla yokuvela nokuphononongwa kwayo, iyakuphepha ukuqamba izikolo ezilahlekileyo, kwaye ihlaziya iisethi zedatha yelizwe ngokuzimeleyo.

## Ukufikelela kulwimi

IsiNgesi sihlala singumthombo ogciniweyo. Amaphepha asekuhlaleni asebenzisa ii-URL ezahlukeneyo kwaye aphinde aqwalaselwe isigama semfundo, amagama ezikolo, izikripthi, isalathiso, kunye nokufikeleleka.

## Qala ukufunda

Ndwendwela [brasa.education](https://brasa.education/) okanye uqale kwiphepha lelizwe lakho kwi-[BRASA World](https://brasa.world/).

## Yiba negalelo

Nceda uchonge iseti yedatha yesikolo esemthethweni, uqinisekise incwadi yesikolo, uphonononge inguqulelo, okanye unike ingxelo ngekhonkco lemfundo eliphelelwe lixesha.

## BRASA namhlanje

BRASA ngoku idibanisa iindawo zamazwe ali-198 neMfundo, uRhulumente kunye noShishino. BRASA Business ineendawo ezili-12 ezidityanisiweyo: intengiso neerisithi, CRM, isitokhwe, abantu noqeqesho, umvuzo neebhanki, ixesha, inkxaso yosapho, umsebenzi wezomthetho, iifomu zikarhulumente, iingxelo nendawo yokusebenza ekwabelwana ngayo. Ikwabandakanya ileja evulekileyo, iinkqubo ezikhokelwayo kunye nokuqhubeka ngaphandle kwe-intanethi.

BRASA Financials idwelisa iinkqubo zentlawulo ezingama-385 ezivela kwiirekhodi ezingama-395, kuquka iinkcazo zemarike ezili-161 kunye neentlobo zentlawulo ezingama-216: imali yeselula, ukudluliselwa kwangoko, QR, izipaji zedijithali, uthungelwano lwemali, ukuthumela imali, open banking, amakhadi asekhaya kunye ne-USSD.

Ukudweliswa akuthethi ukuba sele kuvuliwe. Ababoneleli abanelayisensi bahambisa imali; indlela nganye ifuna ukuphononongwa kwelizwe nomthombo, ilayisensi, ukudityaniswa, uvavanyo lokuhlawula kunye nemvume yokuvelisa.
