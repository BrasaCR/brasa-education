# BRASA Education governed pilot packet

This packet prepares a real institutional pilot. It is not evidence that a pilot has occurred.

## Entry gate

- Institution legal name, jurisdiction and signing authority verified
- Named school leader, safeguarding lead, privacy lead, technical lead and BRASA owner
- Written curriculum scope and participating grades approved
- Teacher reviewers trained with fictional records
- Guardian consent and learner assent approved where applicable
- Withdrawal, correction, deletion, incident and complaint processes tested
- Accessibility accommodations recorded without exposing sensitive learner information
- Baseline measures agreed before enrollment

## Proposed limited pilot

- One institution
- One consenting class
- One term
- One to three subjects
- BRASA Water, Health and Community demonstration pathway plus institution-approved resources
- No high-stakes grading, automated progression or unsupported credential claim

## Measures

| Domain | Baseline | During pilot | Final |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learner completion | Existing comparable completion | Weekly completion and withdrawal | Completion with missing-data disclosure |
| Demonstrated mastery | Existing assessment | Submitted and teacher-verified evidence | Change against agreed rubric |
| Teacher workload | Current minutes per learner | Weekly time diary | Median and range |
| Accessibility | Known accommodations | Barriers and resolutions | Unresolved barriers |
| Reliability | Current tools | Availability, errors, recovery | Incident summary |
| Safety and privacy | Current procedures | Incidents and near misses | Independent review |
| Family understanding | Baseline questionnaire | Support requests | Final questionnaire |

## Stop conditions

- Safeguarding or privacy control fails
- Institution cannot provide qualified teacher review
- Learners cannot withdraw or correct records
- Accessibility barrier prevents equitable participation
- Material source or curriculum error remains uncorrected
- Incident response owner is unavailable

## Publication rule

The report must publish participant counts, consent and withdrawal counts, missing data, adverse events, teacher workload, limitations and the exact authority of any issued evidence. Results may not be generalized beyond the tested population without further evidence.

## Signatures required

- Institutional signing authority
- Safeguarding lead
- Privacy or data-protection owner
- Lead educator
- BRASA accountable owner

Until these names, signatures and dates exist, Gate 8 remains externally blocked and BRASA must describe this only as a pilot-ready packet.
